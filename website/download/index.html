Get SnapView

Starting with SnapView 3.0, the Mac App Store is the only distribution channel. One Universal app supports both Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

Mac App Store

SnapView follows the macOS App Sandbox. macOS may ask for folder access when you open local files, and you stay in control of what the app can access.

Open Mac App Store

Creative Suite Preview

Preview edits and culling suggestions in Creative Suite. Saving edited copies or recipes and applying culling decisions require Supporter Pass. SnapView 3.0 is available on the Mac App Store.

Learn about Supporter Pass

Requirements

  • macOS 14.0 or later
  • Apple Silicon and Intel Mac support in one Universal Mac App Store app
  • RAW, JPG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, supported video files, and ZIP archives
