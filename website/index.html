SnapView is built for people who want simple viewing first, and light organization only when needed.

1. A fast, minimal photo viewer

Open instantly with no setup, and see only what matters. SnapView keeps viewing smooth and enjoyable, not heavy or distracting. (W, 0/9)

2. Scan hundreds at a glance with Grid

Even after a big shooting day, Grid stays easy to scan. See everything at once and jump to the shot you want without breaking flow. (Q, =/-)

3. Open ZIP albums without extracting

Open ZIP archives directly and review the images inside like a normal folder. It keeps archived albums useful without a separate extraction step.

4. Switch into Pro Layout View when the review gets serious

Press Tab for a denser review layout with action and info strips. Ratings, flags, labels, map, histogram, and batch actions stay available without leaving the viewer.

5. Smart Tools to Assist Your Judgment

Pick the best shot instantly and strip away exact duplicates. AI analysis (K), multi-compare (C), and duplicate cleanup tools (Cmd+Shift+D) make your choices fast and precise.
